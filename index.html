Reach rule

  • Reach = the highest objective-level reach in the current view. Each objective's reach is the sum of its daily reach.
  • Meta can't de-duplicate daily reach, so this overstates unique reach over any multi-day period. Use it to compare groups, not as audience size.
  • Frequency = impressions ÷ that reach.

Metric definitions

  • CPM = cost ÷ impressions × 1,000 · CPV = cost ÷ ThruPlays
  • ThruPlay rate = ThruPlays ÷ impressions (image ads count in the base)
  • CPC and CTR use link clicks · CPE = cost ÷ post engagements · ER = engagements ÷ impressions
